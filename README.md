### Hi there, I’m Michael 👋

I’m **Michael Böhme**, theoretical chemist 👨🏻‍🔬 and self-employed software developer 👨🏻‍💻 from **Jena 💚, Germany**. After 12 years of scientific research in computational magnetochemistry at the Friedrich Schiller University in Jena 🧲, I went independent in 2026 to develop and sell my chemistry software **shapeXT**. 💎 You can visit **[www.shapeXT.com](https://www.shapext.com/)** to explore the software, or have a look at my **[personal website](https://www.michael-böhme.de/)** to learn more about my scientific background. 🧪

Besides, **I’ve loved to code** since I was 10 years old. 👦🏻 I learned to program on an old **Commodore C64** 👾 with the help of the great C64 User's Guide. 📖 Nowadays, I mostly work with **C++23**, **Python**, and 3D graphics programming in **Vulkan** 🌋 (plus occasional BASH scripts or gnuplot 📈). My former "secret project" 😎 officially launched in **October 2026**: **[shapeXT](https://www.shapext.com/)** brings high-performance 3D structure visualization, interactive crystallography, and quantum-chemical orbital rendering to Windows, macOS, and Linux! 🚀🖖
